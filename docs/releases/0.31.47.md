### In this version

- **Endgame crises now announce themselves like the end of the world.** The five crisis announcements have been rewritten: the graves open, the frost blackens the harvests, the cities rise against you. Under the title: "The end of days begins".
- **A spell that cannot target a unit now says why.** Web cast on Wraiths used to answer "Spell cancelled", then turn your click into an attack order and complain that the unit flies. Web does catch flying units; Wraiths are incorporeal, and the web passes right through them. That is what the game now says, and the spell stays ready for another target. Every combat spell gives its real reason the same way: immunity, wrong side, already affected, and so on. Web's description now mentions incorporeal units.
