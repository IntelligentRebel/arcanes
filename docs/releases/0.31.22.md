**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/
Or download `arcanes-0.31.22.zip` below, unzip it, and open `arcanes/index.html`.

### First public release

- Two planes, 14 races with their own cities and walls, 14 wizards, 224 spells across five realms and the Arcane.
- Heroes, epic quests, the Book of Legend, a wonder for each race.
- Tactical battles and sieges: ramparts, gates, towers, Wall of Fire.
- An AI that never cheats: difficulty changes how rivals play, not what they get.
- 11 languages, following your browser.

### New in this version

- The offline copy checks GitHub for newer versions at launch and offers to download them (can be turned off in the menu).
- At turn 10, the game offers once to download a backup copy of your game every 10 turns.
