### In this version

- **Rival wizards now defend their cities with archers.** A threatened city raises two ranged defenders before anything else, the best ones it already knows how to train. Garrisons behind walls had nothing to answer flyers and archers with, and fell to armies they should have held. Over 600 test games, the wizard who does this survives to the end of the game 85 % of the time instead of 76 %, and holds one more city on average. Hard and Hardcore.
- An earlier version of the same idea, which had cities put up sawmills and barracks first to unlock better archers, cost more in the early game than it returned and has been dropped. Cities only train what they can already build, and only when a threat is near.
