**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

- **Semi-automatic battles.** Choose it when a battle opens, or switch it on during the battle. On your turn you keep the hand: cast your spells, command any unit you like, then "End turn" and your troops play the rest of the turn on their own. You get the hand back at the start of each of your turns.
- **Spell trades with several spells and gold.** Pick as many spells as you like on each side, and add gold either way. "Balance" sets the gold that makes the deal fair in the other wizard's eyes. You can also buy a spell with gold, or sell one. A wizard never pays more than half of their treasury.
- **Previous and next hero** in the hero sheet, to pass items from one hero to another without closing it.
- **The map stays sharp behind windows.** The background is only darkened now, no longer blurred. The menu offers three settings: sharp and darkened, sharp and barely darkened, or blurred as before.
- The battle log no longer slides under the battle buttons.
