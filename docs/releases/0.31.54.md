**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

**Map and units**
- The thin dark line where the world wraps east to west is gone.
- A ship carrying troops now shows the ship on the map, not one of its passengers.
- "Next unit" no longer bounces between the same two units: a unit you skip waits until the others have had their turn.
- Double-clicking is more forgiving: two clicks within half a second count as one double-click.

**Hero sheet**
- Stats with long bonuses no longer wrap into each other.

**Diplomacy**
- When a wizard offers you a city, "View the offered city" takes you to it; the proposal waits for you underneath and comes back when you are done.

**Saves**
- The folder picker reopens in the last folder you chose.
- Saves you export by hand carry the time in their name, so two exports on the same turn no longer overwrite each other.

**Battles**
- The battle header shows your casting skill left this turn and your mana.
- The battle log is taller, keeps the whole battle, and stays where you scrolled.
- Replaying a battle keeps the log of the previous attempt above the new one.
- "Look at the battlefield again" before the result screen.
- If something goes wrong while a battle is drawn, the battle carries on and an error box stays on screen with a "Copy to report it" button.
