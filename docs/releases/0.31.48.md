### In this version

- **Animated wizard portraits.** The wizards now blink from time to time, and their magic slowly flares and fades: Tsevan's lightning crackles, Brakkon's fire rises behind his shoulders, Célestine's halo brightens, Vorgath's purple flame surges. You see it when choosing your wizard (the one you pick, and the others when you hover over them), in diplomacy, at first contact, in proposals and when you complete a spell. Your own portrait in the top banner stays still, and defeated or banished wizards no longer move. You can turn it off in the menu: "Animated portraits".
