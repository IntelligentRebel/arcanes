### In this version

- **Myrror feels darker.** Rival wizards no longer pick their plane at random: Death wizards (and, to a lesser extent, Chaos wizards) lean toward Myrror, Life wizards toward Arcanus. It is a lean, not a rule: now and then a wizard of Life is born in the world below. The number of wizards on each plane does not change.
