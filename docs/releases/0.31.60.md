### In this version

- **Rival wizards sent to Myrror keep their personality.** They used to lose their own retort (Vorgath his Infernal Power, Brakkon his Chaos Mastery) to make room for Myrran. Now they get Myrran on top, for free: the wizards of the other plane are meant to be stronger, and one of them often ends up as the final threat.
- **Zirrakh, native of Myrror, almost always starts there** (about 9 games out of 10).
- Spell deals offered by other wizards are always at a fair price: when a wizard cannot pay enough gold to balance a deal, it no longer offers it.
