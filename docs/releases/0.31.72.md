### In this version

- **Five area spells**, rare and expensive, each with its icon. They hit everything in the area, your own troops included:
  - Fire Rain (Chaos): 3 × 3 tiles, strength 10 fire;
  - Frost Ring (Nature): the eight tiles around the targeted point, strength 10 cold, the center is spared;
  - Meteor Shower (Chaos, very rare): 3 × 3 tiles, strength 18 fire, wall sections collapse and towers take 15 damage;
  - Plague Cloud (Death): a cloud lasting 3 rounds, strength 5 death on each figure every round, no effect on undead;
  - Holy Dawn (Life): heals 10 HP to each of your units in 3 × 3 tiles.
- Rival wizards and heroes use them too, and avoid burning their own troops.
