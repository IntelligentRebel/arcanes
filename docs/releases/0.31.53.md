### In this version

- The end of a game now shows the wizard it is about. Win by conquest and you see your wizard on the ramparts of the capital, above an endless parade of every people; complete the Spell of Mastery and you see your wizard atop a tower, a pillar of light rising to the heavens. One illustration of each for all 14 wizards.
- If a rival completes the Spell of Mastery, you see that rival's triumph; if you lose your last city, you see your own wizard's fall.
- The illustration is taller on the end screen, so the wizard is no longer cropped.
