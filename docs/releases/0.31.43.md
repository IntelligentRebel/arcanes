**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

- **Half-translated battle lines, in every language but French.** "Barbarian Spearmen are pris in the flames", "Hell Hounds are anéantis". The game writes a French sentence, puts the verb in the plural, then translates the whole thing. The build tool was translating the plural-building step itself, so the sentence came out of it already half English and the real translation no longer matched. All twenty-two of these verb forms are now correct, checked in every language.
- **Save files are named in your language.** `arcanes-thalgren-turn134.json` in English, `zug134` in German, `turno134` in Spanish.
- **Units that vanished in combat.** A creature that arrived while an animation was playing had no position on screen yet, and was simply not drawn until the animation ended. It is now drawn on its own tile.
- **Clicks lost to a shaky hand.** The map counted a click as a drag at the slightest tremble while the button was down, and threw it away. What counts now is where the button comes back up. Fast double-clicks were the ones being lost.
