### In this version

- **Troops stopped by an encounter keep their route.** It stays drawn on the map, and "Resume route" sets them off again toward the same destination.
- **Spell offers from other wizards.** They now propose deals with gold too: several spells, buying one of yours, or selling you one. Before, they almost never offered anything.
- **Recall to your summoning circle.** Word of Recall and Recall Hero now bring the unit back to the city of your summoning circle, as in Master of Magic (your Fortress until you move the circle).
- Exporting the game by hand now says so, with the file name.
- Road plans only go over land: flying engineers no longer plan roads across the sea.
- Volunteers found on a small island where you have no city now join you at your nearest city, instead of being stranded there.
- "Explore" on a unit with no movement left now says exploring resumes next turn.
