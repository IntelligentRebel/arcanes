### In this version

- **Endgame crises.** After turn 200, when one wizard outweighs the second one and a half times for five turns in a row, an omen passes, and five turns later a crisis drawn at random rises against that leader (it threatens everyone on its way). If a Legendary Demon has been slain, it comes sooner. One per game, scaled on the leader's strength:
  - **The Lich Lord**: his dead march on the leader, swelled by the dead of every battle. Struck down, he rises again while his phylactery stands; each fall gives away a clue to its hiding place.
  - **The Forgotten Archmage**: a citadel bursts out of the ground. His counter-magic makes the leader's spells cost half again as much; he tears away mana, strikes cities and sends out creatures.
  - **The Great Winter**: frost spreads from the Heart of Winter over the leader's lands and kills the harvests. Breaking the Heart thaws everything.
  - **The Node Eater**: it devours magic nodes, the leader's first, and grows with each one.
  - **The Prophet**: he stirs unrest in the leader's cities, raises rebel armies, and tears cities away from the empire.
- Destroying the crisis (its site, or its monster) brings a large reward to whoever does it. If its prey falls, the crisis dies out on its own.
- An option at game setup turns endgame crises off.
