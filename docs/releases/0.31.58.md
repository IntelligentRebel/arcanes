### In this version

- **Enchanted roads are no longer free.** A tile of enchanted road (from the Enchant Road spell, and every road on Myrror) now costs a quarter of a movement point: twice as fast as an ordinary road. Before, it cost nothing, so an army could cross a whole road network in one turn. A spearman now covers 1 tile off-road, 2 on a road, 4 on an enchanted road.
- **Rival wizards lead peoples that match their magic.** Life wizards lean toward High Men and Halflings, Death wizards toward Dark Elves, Chaos wizards toward Orcs and Draconians, Nature wizards toward High Elves and Trolls. A lean, not a rule.
