**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

- **Customisable keys.** Menu > Keys: every map and combat shortcut can be changed. A key already in use moves to the order you just changed.
- **Quick save.** F5 or Ctrl+S saves instead of reloading the page, F9 loads that save back. Closing or reloading the page with unsaved moves asks for confirmation.
- **Patrol (Z).** A stack guards an area of 2 to 5 tiles. At the start of your turn, it attacks on its own any enemy that entered it, if it can reach it this turn and its chances are at least good. Otherwise it holds its position, then returns to its post.
- **Enemy moves.** At the start of your turn, arrows show what your troops saw moving during the other players' turns. Y shows them again.
- **Markers (K).** A pin and a note on the hovered tile, also shown on the minimap.
- **Search (Ctrl+F).** A city, hero, known place or marker, by name: the map centers on it.
- **Casting queue.** While a spell is being cast, another spell from the spellbook can be queued. It begins as soon as the way is clear, using the casting skill left that turn.
- **Quick spells in combat.** Your latest combat spells, at the start of the action bar, can be cast again with keys 1 to 9.
- **Item comparison.** Hovering over an item in the vault shows how it would change the hero's stats, in place of the item worn.
- **Log filters.** Battles, Cities, Magic, Diplomacy, Others: each category hides with a click.
