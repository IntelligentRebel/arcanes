### In this version

More from the first players' feedback.

- **Game pace.** A new game can be Quick, Normal or Epic. In Quick, buildings, units, research and spells cast on the map cost a third less, and cities grow faster; in Epic they cost half as much again, and cities grow more slowly. Income, upkeep, movement and combat spells do not change. The Crisis and the endgame crises follow the pace.
- **Automatic battles** can run fast (as before), medium or slow: Menu ▸ Automatic battles.
- **City screen:** previous and next city buttons, without going back to the map.
- **Log:** drag its handle to move it, its corner to resize it (double click the handle to put it back), and choose in the menu whether the newest line is at the bottom or at the top.
- **Controls:** the welcome screen now shows them in a box of their own, and the "double click to move" reminder comes back until you have moved a unit once.
- **No more fallback graphics.** If the high-definition sheets cannot be downloaded, the game no longer starts with rough placeholders: the loading screen says so and offers to retry.
- **Fog seam.** Where the world wraps east to west, the fog could show a hard vertical edge on some graphics cards. It is now drawn in one piece.
- An artifact power named like its ability is no longer shown twice ("Regeneration: Regeneration").
