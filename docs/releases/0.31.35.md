### In this version

Three endgame crises tuned after a series of test games:
- **The Node Eater** now devours only the leader's nodes, then marches on its cities. Before, it mostly ate everyone else's nodes and barely slowed the leader down.
- **The Prophet**: a city that secedes now keeps a rebel garrison and must be retaken by force.
- **The Forgotten Archmage** strikes less often (a wave every 10 turns instead of 7, a spell every 6 instead of 4). The leader loses the lead just as often, but is wiped out far less (19% of these crises instead of 29%).
