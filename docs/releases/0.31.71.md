**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

- **Twenty spells from Heroes of Might and Magic**, adapted to Arcanes, each with its own icon:
  - on the battlefield: Force Field (Nature), Fire Curtain and Fire Mines (Chaos), Mist (Sorcery). Lines of three tiles turn with **R**; your mines stay hidden from the enemy;
  - placement: Teleport and Gust (Sorcery). A Wall of Fire closes a city to the attacker's Teleport;
  - control: Slow and Mass Slow (Nature), Shackles and Twilight (Death), Magic Mirror (Sorcery);
  - trading blows: Chain Lightning (Chaos, it can hit your own troops), Fire Shield (Chaos), Martyr and Divine Vengeance (Life), Sacrifice and Vampirism (Death), Clone (Sorcery);
  - on the map: Dimension Door (Sorcery) and Town Portal (Arcane).
- Rival wizards and heroes cast them too: no cheating, they only use what they see.
