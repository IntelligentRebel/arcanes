### In this version

- **A rival's fall is now an event.** When a wizard's fortress is taken and he is banished, and when a wizard loses his last city and is gone for good, the game says so in the middle of the screen, with a full illustration of that wizard: twenty-eight new paintings, two for each of the fourteen wizards. It tells you who did it, you or another wizard. A wizard you have never met stays anonymous.
- **Wizards screen:** defeated wizards now sit at the bottom of the list, below the ones still in the game.
