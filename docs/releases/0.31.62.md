### In this version

- **The strongest wizard of the other plane comes after you** (Hard and Hardcore). As soon as you set foot on his plane, or around turn 120 at the latest, he hunts you: his expeditions aim at your cities, he learns to open rifts, he refuses peace, and a Planar Seal no longer holds him back for long: he tries to break it.
- **The end-game crisis spares that boss.** If he leads the game, no crisis weakens him on your behalf: bringing him down is up to you.
