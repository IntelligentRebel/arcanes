**Play in your browser:** https://intelligentrebel.github.io/arcanes/

### In this version

- **Neutral creatures no longer retreat.** The guardians of a node or a lair, marauding monsters, the waves out of the Breach: they are there to hold what they hold, and now fight to the death. They were running from a lost battle like any army, only to be ridden down.
- **Retreats that killed everyone.** Even for wizards' troops, fleeing was decided on the average case and ignored the tail: two foot soldiers chased by pursuers of equal speed are both caught one time in four. Across 600 test battles, 56 of the 175 retreats ended with the whole side dead. Fleeing is now refused above a one-in-five chance of losing everyone. Troops faster than their pursuers, and heroes, still get away. Wipes drop from 56 to 3 in the same 600 battles.
- **Placeholder graphics removed for good.** Every image in the game is now its own artwork: the old fallback tile sheet, its rendering path and its last few icons are gone. The download is 0.6 MB lighter and loads one file less.
