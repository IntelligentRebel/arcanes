### In this version

- **Retreats that killed everyone.** Defenders of a node or of a stack in the open would run even when the pursuit was almost certain to ride every one of them down: across 600 test battles, 56 of the 175 retreats ended with the whole side dead. Fleeing is now refused above a one-in-five chance of losing everyone, so they sell their lives dearly instead. Troops faster than their pursuers, and heroes, still get away as before. Retreats that wipe out the side drop from 56 to 3 in the same 600 battles.
- Battle scenery no longer falls back to a placeholder tree.
