### In this version

- **Founding a city needs movement left**, as in Master of Magic and Civilization. Settlers that arrive out of movement found the city next turn.
- **Battlefield rivers redone:** a single connected river, two tiles wide, winding between the two armies. It can be crossed in two places, over a bridge or through a ford. Flying and swimming units cross wherever they like.
