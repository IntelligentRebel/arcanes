### In this version

- **Open Rift is no longer open to everyone.** It used to show up among the research options from the first turns, letting anyone slip past the guardians of the Sorcery Towers. Now it is offered only to the wizards of one plane, and only once a wizard from the other plane outweighs the strongest of them by 1.5 times (from turn 60). It then joins their research options on top of the usual eight and stays there. It becomes uncommon and costs 500 research points instead of 300. The leader can no longer get it through spell trading.
