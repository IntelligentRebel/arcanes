### In this version

Fixes from the first players' feedback.

- **First load.** The game used to download about 125 MB of images before showing anything, with no sign of progress. It now shows a loading screen with the progress, opens the title screen after about 36 MB, and fetches the battle animations (90 MB) in the background: until they arrive, units stand still.
- **Missing graphics.** When a high-definition sheet failed to download, the game silently fell back to rough placeholder graphics for the whole session, and some spell icons went missing. Downloads are now retried up to three times, and if one still fails, a banner says so and offers to reload.
- **French left in other languages.** About eighty texts stayed in French whatever the language: the battle forecast ("uncertain", "favorable"), the choice to annex a conquered city, the chronicle's sentences, diplomatic moods, intelligence quality, artifact bonuses, unit status tags in battle, and more. They are now translated in all ten languages.
- **Menu settings.** Toggling a setting reopened the menu scrolled back to the top, so the next click landed on another line, and a double-click toggled a setting twice. The menu now stays where it was, and a double-click counts once.
- **High-contrast themes** (Windows, Android). Chosen picks and other active choices could not be told apart. The game now keeps its own colors, chosen picks carry a check mark, and active choices are outlined.
- **Freezes.** A display error could stop the screen from refreshing for good, and an error while your troops carried out their orders could leave the waiting screen up forever. The game now carries on and reports the error.
- The chronicle's "Curves" tab is now called "Graphs" in English.
