### In this version

- **Endgame crises scale with the lead.** The further ahead the leader is, the stronger the crisis sent after them. Leading by 1.5 to 2 times the runner-up: the Prophet, the Node Eater or the Great Winter. Twice the runner-up or more: the Great Winter, the Lich Lord or the Forgotten Archmage. Twice the runner-up and at least as strong as all the others combined: the Forgotten Archmage.
- **New arcane spell, Open Rift** (common, available to all wizards): tears the veil beneath your troops and opens a rift between the two planes, which they can cross like a Sorcery Tower. It wears the veil thin, and creatures from Myrror eventually come out of it.
- **The other plane strikes back** (Hard and Hardcore). When the leader was born on the other plane, the wizards well behind them (at most 0.8 times their strength) take them as their target from turn 60: their expeditions go for the leader's cities, set out again after 8 turns instead of 25 so reinforcements follow, also cross through rifts, and when no useful crossing exists, they learn and cast Open Rift beneath an army ready to go through.
