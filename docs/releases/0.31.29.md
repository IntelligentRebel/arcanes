### In this version

- When a rival steals one of your magic nodes, you now learn it at the start of your turn, in the middle of the screen, with an illustration for each kind of node.
- Disenchant Area (and Disenchant True), cast on a rival's node, breaks the guard of its Guardian Spirit, as in Master of Magic: wizards without Life magic now have an answer to it.
