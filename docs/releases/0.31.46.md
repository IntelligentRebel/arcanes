### In this version

- **The Prophet bites harder.** Of the endgame crises, he was the one a dominant wizard shrugged off most easily. His preaching now reaches the whole empire of his target, not only the cities around him, and cities break away sooner. Measured twice, on 230 then 450 test games: a wizard far ahead when the Prophet rises now stays first about seven times in ten instead of eight.
- Rival wizards were tried with city walls and defense towers built much earlier. It slowed their start and protected them no better, so they keep building them as before.
