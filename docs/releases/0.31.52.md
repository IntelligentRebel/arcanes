### In this version

- The monster of an end-game crisis (Prophet, Node Eater, Lich Lord) is now stronger than its prey's best champion: a lone hero, even the best one, can no longer bring it down. It takes an army. The monster gains mostly endurance, and its strength is checked again every 5 turns as your heroes grow.
- The crisis drawn now also weighs the leader's armies: a wizard five times stronger than the second by force of arms, but only 1.6 times richer, used to draw a mild crisis such as the Prophet. Now he faces the strongest one, the Forgotten Archmage.
- A defeated crisis gets its own illustration: the fallen Prophet, the crumbling Lich Lord, the collapsing citadel, the thaw, the dying Node Eater.
